Arab Ice-cream

Description

Arabic ice cream, known traditionally as Booza (or Būẓah), is a dense, stretchy,
and chewy frozen dairy dessert from the Middle East

Ingredients

  • Sahlab (also spelled Salep)
  • Mastic Gum (known as Mistika)
  • Milk and Cream
  • Sugar
  • Floral Waters
  • Pistachios

Steps

  1. Warm the Milk
  2. Add Sahlab
  3. Add Mastic and Thicken
  4. Incorporate Cream
  5. Freeze and Stretch (The Booza Method)
  6. Form and Chill
  7. Serve
