British rice

Description

Rice in Britain is a major dietary staple,
with roughly 88% of UK households purchasing it and average consumption sitting at about 5.6 kg per person annually.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Fish or eggs
  • Aromatics & Spices
  • Toppings

Steps

  1. Measure the rice
  2. Rinse the grains
  3. Combine with water
  4. Bring to a boil
  5. Simmer on low
  6. Rest off the heat
  7. Fluff and serve
