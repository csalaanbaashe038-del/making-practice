Turkish meat

Description

Turkish cuisine is a rich culinary tradition blending Mediterranean,
Middle Eastern, and Central Asian influences developed over a thousand years of Ottoman heritage.

Ingredients

  • Pul Biber (Aleppo Pepper)
  • Sumac
  • Biber Salçasi (Red Pepper Paste)
  • Pomegranate Molasses (Nar Ekşisi)
  • Dried Mint (Kuru Nane)
  • Mahleb (Mahlep)
  • Nigella Seeds (Çörek Otu)
  • Tahini (Tahin)

Steps

  1. Chop the vegetables
  2. Sauté the base
  3. Add the tomatoes
  4. Scramble the eggs
  5. Process the aromatics
  6. Mix the meat
  7. Shape and top
  8. Serve
